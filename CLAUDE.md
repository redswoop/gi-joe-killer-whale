### Vane mechanism (section "vane mechanism" in shroud.scad)

Designed 2026-09-08, with `check.scad` (collision pairs at steer 0/±30, tilt 0/±20), `print_layout.scad`, `hinge_coupon.scad`, STLs in `stl/`.

- Frame: Z = duct axis, Y = toy vertical with **+Y (tab side) = the toy's BOTTOM**. The tie bar lives at the +Y end. It was flipped once.
- The real part: root bar (pegs at both ends, 2 ribbed panels, 2 axle holes) + trapezoid plate (~15 deep at top, ~25 at bottom, 2 ribbed panels, a bulbed pin at the bottom trailing corner) joined by a living hinge; tie bar with eye ends snaps over the two pins; two tapered slats (~52 at the rod, ~40 at the edge, ~17 deep) pivot on rods through the bar holes at y ≈ -34.6 and 13.6. Dims are photo estimates scaled from the 92 mm bar; caliper numbers go into `fin_depth_*`, `slat_*`, `slat_y`.
- Hinge: two short nubs (root-plate-root, 4 mm knuckles, ~13 mm) at y = -34 and 36, barrel 4.2, round pin 1.4 on the root, teardrop holes in the plate knuckles (a diamond pin in a diamond hole cannot rotate). Barrels on the INSIDE face (`hinge_in = true`). Armen twice asked for smaller hinges ("fugly huge"), then to hide them: keep hinge visuals minimal.
- Linkage is a four-bar, not a parallelogram (pins ~1 mm outboard of the hinge axis, mirrored): `solve_left()` bisects the left plate's angle; 29.2 deg left for 30 deg right. Fin touches a slat at 40 deg steer (design range ±30).
- Prints: two vane prints, a tie bar, two slats (one combined flat print is impossible). Slat rods (59 mm) get sprung into the bar holes before the vanes drop into the shroud pockets. Vanes: `print_face` = "outer" or "inner"; Armen prints the vanes VERTICALLY (standing on the -Y end; "details on the vanes don't come out great printed flat").
- Strut-to-shroud history: v1 external T-head clips (ugly), v2 internal bracket + flexing hook ("pops out with any pressure"), v3 twist lock (git be6d171: arc ribs on the bore wall at 3/9 o'clock swept with `arc_sweep()` hulls, shoes with a groove on the bar ends, stop block + detent), v4 (current, 2026-09-10) EARS IN POCKETS: bar ends turn up into 2 mm curved ears (`ear()`), boss on the bore wall (`boss_pad()`) with a blind pocket open at the BASE (`pocket()`), inserted from the base side so the hull retains it; `ear_clr` untested, coupon 0.1/0.2/0.3; bar prints standing on an ear. Armen rejected a dovetail version (git 9d956e5) as "way over engineered", "giant wedge": simplest geometry that does the job, inserted from the toy side.
- Tab: the Whale's opening is a square for the tab head then a channel the stem slides along. `tab_coupon.scad` prints four 108 deg arcs with the L tab at several grips. Settled 2026-09-10: tab_grip 0.7 (0.8 tight, 0.5 loose), tab_stem_extra 0.2. Armen draws the tab as `|__` ('|' the stem, which he calls 'A', '__' the foot).
- Coupon round 1 (2026-09-08): hinge 0.35/0.45 good, 0.25 bad; fin barrel fused to the bar top -> `hinge_lift = 0.6`; strut snap loose -> `hook_preload = 0.1` (check.scad overrides it to 0 so the interference doesn't read as a collision; a flush lip shows as a 0.00 mm3 "collision", expected).

## windscreen/ — cockpit windscreen

Started 2026-09-13. Three flat 1 mm clear PETG panels: back 60 at the base / 65 at the top (V from the front), sides 40 / 44, height 10, sheets 1 mm (one wall on the 0.6 nozzle). Sides lean out 2.5 each; the side's free edge is square in the flat sheet, so the back rakes forward by 4. The 60 mm panel is the FRONT (the C opens toward the pilot). Panels are defined by 3D outer-face corner points, built as oversized slabs clipped by half-space planes (mitred corners, horizontal top rim, square free edge); `panel_place()`/`panel_unplace()` are a frame matrix and its inverse. Sheets print outer face down.

Base: 5 wide C rail, grooves cut by the panels themselves grown by `groove_clr`, three 5x5x2 deck tabs hang below, side tabs 5 mm from the free edge, cosmetic vertical lines on the outer faces. Printed upside down. Optional top frame (`show_top`); Armen has not said whether its size is right.

Why: max clarity, so the clear parts print flat as separate sheets and an opaque base sets the angles. Groove clearance still needs the coupon (`stl/coupon.stl` PLA + `stl/strip.stl` PETG). Tab size was never measured against the deck. Keep heights/angles derived from the corner points, don't type angles.
